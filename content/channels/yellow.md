* Experiment:
  * Location: 22.1N, 159.8W
  * Date recorded: July 2011
  * Center frequency: f<sub>c</sub> = 13 kHz
  * Bandwidth: B = 6.25 kHz
  * Symbol rate: R = 6.25 ksym/s
  * Distance: d = 3 km and 7 km
  * Tx/Rx/water depth for the 24-element arrays: d<sub>T</sub>/d<sub>R</sub>/d<sub>w</sub> = 50 m/50 m/100 m
  * Tx/Rx/water depth for the 16-element arrays: d<sub>T</sub>/d<sub>R</sub>/d<sub>w</sub> = 50 m/8.6-65 m/100 m
  * Mobility: moored
  * Receiving arrays: four vertical arrays (elements numbered bottom to top)
    * System 1: M = 24 elements, &#8467; = 5 cm spacing, at d = 3 km
    * System 2: M = 24 elements, &#8467; = 20 cm spacing, at d = 7 km
    * System 3: M = 16 elements, &#8467; = 3.75 m spacing, at d = 3 km
    * System 4: M = 16 elements, &#8467; = 3.75 m spacing, at d = 7 km
* Parameters:
  * Sampling rate in delay: f<sub>s</sub> = 2R
  * Total delay coverage: up to 56 ms (15 to 56 ms, depending on the file)
  * Sampling rate in time: R/100 for the channel response, 2R for the phase/delay
  * Total time coverage: 51 to 52 seconds
