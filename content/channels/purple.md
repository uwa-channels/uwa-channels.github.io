  A 12.5 kHz shallow-water channel from the SPACE'08 experiment off Martha's Vineyard, with cross and vertical receiving arrays at ranges from 60 m to 1 km.
---

* Experiment:
  * Location: 41.2N, 70.3W
  * Date recorded: October 2008
  * Center frequency: f<sub>c</sub> = 12.5 kHz
  * Bandwidth: B = 6.6 kHz (9.2-15.8 kHz)
  * Symbol rate: R = 10<sup>7</sup>/1536 sym/s (approximately 6.5 ksym/s)
  * Distance: d = 60 m (systems 1 and 2), 200 m (systems 3 and 4), and 1 km (system 5)
  * Tx/Rx/water depth: d<sub>T</sub>/d<sub>R</sub>/d<sub>w</sub> = 11 m/10 m/15 m
  * Mobility: moored
  * Receiving arrays: five receive systems in three configurations, each with its top element about 3.3 m above the seabed
    * Systems 1 and 2: cross, 16 elements in each leg (M = 32 in total), &#8467; = 3.75 cm spacing (numbered top to bottom, then left to right), 60 m southeast and southwest of the transmitter
    * Systems 3 and 4: vertical, M = 24 elements, &#8467; = 5 cm spacing (numbered top to bottom), 200 m southeast and southwest of the transmitter
    * System 5: vertical, M = 12 elements, &#8467; = 12 cm spacing (numbered top to bottom), 1 km southeast of the transmitter
  * Files: `purple_1` ... `purple_5` are systems 1 to 5, and `purple_6` ... `purple_10` and `purple_11` ... `purple_15` repeat the five systems
* Parameters:
  * Sampling rate in delay: f<sub>s</sub> = 2R
  * Total delay coverage: up to 33 ms (23 to 33 ms, depending on the file)
  * Sampling rate in time: R/100 for the channel response, 2R for the phase/delay
  * Total time coverage: 53 seconds
