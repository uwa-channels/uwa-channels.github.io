Use channels measured at sea to test underwater communication signals. The library provides channel impulse responses, ambient noise models, and code for replaying your signals through the recorded channels.

Browse the channels and noise models, or follow the user's guide to get started. If you use the library in your work, please cite the accompanying paper.
