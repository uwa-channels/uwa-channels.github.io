Welcome to the Underwater Acoustic Channel Library. This library contains impulse responses of communication channels recorded during different at-sea experiments, as well as the accompanying noise models. It also contains a user's guide that explains how to download the channels, or how to directly apply the channels to a signal of your choice. Click on the entries below to explore the library. If you use the library in your work, please cite the accompanying paper.
